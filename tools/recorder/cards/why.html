11Why a blockchain?

Because the core risk is a double-spend.

01Three parties, three jurisdictionsProducer in Türkiye or India, verifier and importer in the EU. No neutral database operator exists between them.
02The same tonnes, claimed twiceMinted once (ERC-1155). Shipping more than was verified reverts, and certified periods can't overlap.
03Sign-offs that hold for yearsPublic, timestamped attestations with a post-quantum ML-DSA-65 signature next to ECDSA.
04Plant data is commercially sensitiveOnly salted Merkle roots and hashes go on-chain. Files are disclosed selectively.
The chain doesn't make a meter reading true. That's why an accredited verifier and anomaly checks sit in front of it.
